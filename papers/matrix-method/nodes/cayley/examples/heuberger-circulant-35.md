# Main Content

Consider the [[cayley.circulant-heuberger-matrix|circulant Heuberger-matrix construction]] for
\[
X=C_{35}(6,10)
=
\operatorname{Cay}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}_{35},\{\pm6,\pm10\}\bigr).
\]

Define
\[
\psi\colon\mathbb{Z}^2\longrightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{35}
\]
by
\[
\psi(e_1)=6+35\mathbb{Z},
\qquad
\psi(e_2)=10+35\mathbb{Z}.
\]

The vectors
\[
y_1=(5,4)^t
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
y_2=(0,7)^t
\]
belong to $\ker\psi$, since
\[
5(6)+4(10)=70\equiv0\pmod{35}
\]
and
\[
7(10)=70\equiv0\pmod{35}.
\]

The subgroup generated by $y_1$ and $y_2$ has index
\[
\left|
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
4&7
\end{pmatrix}
\right|
=35.
\]
Because $\psi$ is surjective, $\ker\psi$ also has index $35$.
The containment
\[
\langle y_1,y_2\rangle\subseteq\ker\psi
\]
and equality of indices therefore imply
\[
\ker\psi=\langle y_1,y_2\rangle.
\]

Consequently,
\[
M_X=
\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
4&7
\end{pmatrix}
\]
is a Heuberger matrix for $X$, and
\[
C_{35}(6,10)
\cong
\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
4&7
\end{pmatrix}^{\mathrm{SACG}}.
\]
