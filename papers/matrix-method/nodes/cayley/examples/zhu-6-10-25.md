# Main Content

Consider the [[cayley.integer-distance-heuberger-matrix|integer-distance Heuberger-matrix construction]] for
\[
W=\operatorname{Cay}\bigl(\mathbb{Z},\{\pm6,\pm10,\pm25\}\bigr).
\]

Choose the signed generator list $(-6,10,25)$, which determines the same symmetric generating set.
Define
\[
\phi\colon\mathbb{Z}^3\longrightarrow\mathbb{Z}
\]
by
\[
\phi(e_1)=-6,
\qquad
\phi(e_2)=10,
\qquad
\phi(e_3)=25.
\]

The vectors
\[
y_1=(5,-12,6)^t
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
y_2=(0,5,-2)^t
\]
belong to $\ker\phi$, since
\[
5(-6)-12(10)+6(25)=0
\]
and
\[
5(10)-2(25)=0.
\]

They generate $\ker\phi$.
Equivalently, the generalized cross product of $y_1$ and $y_2$ is
\[
y_1\times y_2=(-6,10,25)^t,
\]
which is primitive and records the chosen generator list.

Therefore
\[
M_W=
\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
-12&5\\
6&-2
\end{pmatrix}
\]
is a Heuberger matrix for $W$, and
\[
W\cong
\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
-12&5\\
6&-2
\end{pmatrix}^{\mathrm{SACG}}.
\]

## Sign convention

The same graph is generated by $(6,10,25)$ because the Cayley generating set contains both signs.
However, the displayed columns are relations for the signed list $(-6,10,25)$, not for $(6,10,25)$.
