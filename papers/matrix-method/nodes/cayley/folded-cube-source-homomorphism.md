# Main Content

Let $X$ be a loopless, nonbipartite cube-like graph.
Then there is an odd integer $\ell\geq3$ and a graph homomorphism $Q_{\ell-1}^d\to X$.
In particular, $\ell-1$ is even and $\chi(Q_{\ell-1}^d)\leq\chi(X)$.

## Proof

Write $X=\operatorname{Cay}(\mathbb{Z}_2^n,S)$.
By [[cayley.connected-component-reduction]], replacing $\mathbb{Z}_2^n$ by the subgroup generated by $S$ selects one connected component of $X$.
Since all components are mutually isomorphic, we may assume that $S$ generates the ambient group.

Enumerate the generators as $S=\{s_1,\dots,s_m\}$.
Consider the surjective homomorphism $\pi\colon\mathbb{Z}^m\to\mathbb{Z}_2^n$ defined by $\pi(e_i)=s_i$.
Since every element of $\mathbb{Z}_2^n$ has order at most $2$, the kernel of $\pi$ contains $2e_1,\dots,2e_m$.
Consequently, $X$ has a Heuberger matrix of the form
\[M_X = \left( A\;\middle|\;2I_m\right).\]

Using the column operations in [[sacg.isomorphisms.matrix-isomorphisms]], we may reduce every entry of $A$ modulo $2$.
Thus
\[X \cong \left(A'\;\middle|\;2I_m\right)^{\mathrm{SACG}},\]
where every entry of $A'$ belongs to $\{0,1\}$.

The columns of $2I_m$ have even column sums.
Because $X$ is not bipartite, [[sacg.colorings.bipartite-criterion]] implies that some column $y$ of $A'$ has odd column sum.

Let $\operatorname{supp}(y) = \{i_1,\dots,i_{\ell}\}$.
Since $y$ is a zero-one vector, its column sum is $\ell$.
Therefore, $\ell$ is odd.

Consider the graph $Y$ represented by $\left(w_{\ell}^t\;\middle|\;2I_{\ell}\right)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_Y$.
By [[cayley.cube-like-graphs]], $Y\cong Q_{\ell-1}^d$.

Insert zero rows into the matrix for $Y$ so that its $j$th row occupies row $i_j$.
This gives the graph homomorphism
\[
\left(w_{\ell}^t\;\middle|\;2I_{\ell}\right)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_Y \xrightarrow{\circledcirc}\left(y\;2e_{i_1}\;\cdots\;2e_{i_{\ell}}\right)^{\mathrm{SACG}}.
\]
Appending the remaining columns of $A'$ and $2I_m$ gives a second graph homomorphism
\[
\left(y\;2e_{i_1}\;\cdots\;2e_{i_{\ell}}\right)^{\mathrm{SACG}}
\xrightarrow{\circledcirc}
\left(A'\;\middle|\;2I_m\right)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_X.
\]
These operations are instances of the homomorphisms collected in [[sacg.homomorphisms.matrix-homomorphisms]].

Their composition gives $Q_{\ell-1}^d\cong Y\to X$.
If $\ell=1$, then $y=e_{i_1}$ belongs to $\ker\pi$.
Hence $s_{i_1}=\pi(e_{i_1})=0$, so $X$ has a loop.
Because $X$ is loopless, we must have $\ell\geq3$.
Finally, [[graph.homomorphism-chromatic-number]] gives $\chi(Q_{\ell-1}^d)\leq\chi(X)$.
