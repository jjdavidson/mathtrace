# Main Content

Let $X$ and $Y$ be graphs. A *graph homomorphism* from $X$ to $Y$ is a map $\psi\colon V(X)\to V(Y)$
such that whenever $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in $X$, the vertices $\psi(u)$ and $\psi(v)$ are adjacent in $Y$.
