# Main Content

Let $X$ and $X'$ be standardized abelian Cayley graphs with Heuberger matrices $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.
If $M_{X'}$ is obtained by multiplying one row of $M_X$ by $-1$, then $X\cong X'$.

## Proof

Suppose that $M_X$ has $m$ rows and that the $j$th row is multiplied by $-1$.
Let $D$ be the diagonal $m\times m$ matrix whose $j$th diagonal entry is $-1$ and whose remaining diagonal entries are $1$.
Then $M_{X'}=DM_X$.

Let $H_X$ and $H_{X'}$ be the subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}^m$ generated by the columns of $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.
Since the columns of $M_{X'}$ are the images under $D$ of the columns of $M_X$, we have $H_{X'}=D(H_X)$.
By [[sacg.matrix-realization]], the vertex sets of $X$ and $X'$ are $\mathbb{Z}^m/H_X$ and $\mathbb{Z}^m/H_{X'}$, respectively.

Define $\overline{D}:\mathbb{Z}^m/H_X\to\mathbb{Z}^m/H_{X'}$ by
\[\overline{D}(x + H_X) = Dx + H_{X'}.\]
If $x+H_X = y + H_X$, then $x-y\in H_X$.
Consequently, $D(x-y)\in D(H_X)=H_{X'}$, so
\[Dx + H_{X'} = Dy + H_{X'}.\]
Thus, $\overline{D}$ is well-defined.

Since $D^{-1}=D$, the induced map $\overline{D}$ is a group isomorphism.
Moreover,
\[
De_k =
\begin{cases}
    -e_j & \text{if } k=j,\\
    e_k & \text{if } k\neq j.
\end{cases}
\]
Therefore, $D\bigl(\{\pm e_1,\ldots,\pm e_m\}\bigr) = \{\pm e_1,\ldots,\pm e_m\}$.
It follows that $\overline{D}$ preserves adjacency.

Hence, $\overline{D}$ is a graph isomorphism and $X\cong X'$.
