# Main Content

Let $X$ and $Y$ be standardized abelian Cayley graphs with Heuberger matrices $M_X$ and $M_Y$, respectively.
Let $Z$ be the standardized abelian Cayley graph represented by

\[
M_X\oplus M_Y=
\begin{pmatrix}
  M_X & 0\\
  0 & M_Y
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then $Z\cong X\square Y$.

## Proof

Suppose that $M_X$ has $m$ rows and $M_Y$ has $n$ rows.
Let $H_X\leq\mathbb{Z}^m$ and $H_Y\leq\mathbb{Z}^n$ be the subgroups generated by the columns of $M_X$ and $M_Y$, respectively.

The columns of $M_X\oplus M_Y$ generate $H_X\times H_Y$ in $\mathbb{Z}^m\times\mathbb{Z}^n$.
By [[sacg.matrix-realization]], the vertex group of $Z$ is therefore $(\mathbb{Z}^m\times\mathbb{Z}^n)/(H_X\times H_Y)$.

Define $\Phi$ by
\[\Phi\bigl((H_X\times H_Y)+(u,v)\bigr)=(H_X+u,H_Y+v).\]
This is a well-defined group isomorphism from the vertex group of $Z$ to $(\mathbb{Z}^m/H_X)\times(\mathbb{Z}^n/H_Y)$.
Under $\Phi$, a standard generator from the first $m$ coordinates changes only the $X$-coordinate along an edge of $X$.
A standard generator from the last $n$ coordinates changes only the $Y$-coordinate along an edge of $Y$.
These are precisely the adjacencies in the box product $X\square Y$.
Therefore, $\Phi$ is a graph isomorphism and $Z\cong X\square Y$.
