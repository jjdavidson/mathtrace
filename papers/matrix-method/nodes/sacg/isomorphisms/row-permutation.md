# Main Content

Let $X$ and $X'$ be standardized abelian Cayley graphs with Heuberger matrices $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.
If $M_{X'}$ is obtained by permuting the rows of $M_X$, then $X\cong X'$.

## Proof

Suppose that $M_X$ has $m$ rows.
Let $P$ be the permutation matrix corresponding to the given row permutation, so that
\[M_{X'}=PM_X.\]

Let $H_X$ and $H_{X'}$ be the subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}^m$ generated by the columns of $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.
Since the columns of $M_{X'}$ are the images under $P$ of the columns of $M_X$, we have $H_{X'}=P(H_X)$.
By [[sacg.matrix-realization]], the vertex sets of $X$ and $X'$ are $\mathbb{Z}^m/H_X$ and $\mathbb{Z}^m/H_{X'}$, respectively.

Define $\overline{P}:\mathbb{Z}^m/H_X\to\mathbb{Z}^m/H_{X'}$ by
\[\overline{P}(x+H_X) = Px + H_{X'}.\]
If $x + H_X = y + H_X$, then $x-y\in H_X$.
Consequently, $P(x-y)\in P(H_X)=H_{X'}$, so 
\[Px + H_{X'} = Py + H_{X'}.\]
Thus, $\overline{P}$ is well-defined.

Since $P$ is an automorphism of $\mathbb{Z}^m$, the induced map $\overline{P}$ is a group isomorphism.

There is a permutation $\pi$ of $\{1,\ldots,m\}$ such that $Pe_k=e_{\pi(k)}$ for every $k$.
Therefore,
\[P\bigl(\{\pm e_1,\ldots,\pm e_m\}\bigr) = \{\pm e_1,\ldots,\pm e_m\}.\]
It follows that $\overline{P}$ preserves adjacency.
Hence, $\overline{P}$ is a graph isomorphism and $X\cong X'$.
