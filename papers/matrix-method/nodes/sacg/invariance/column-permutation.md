# Main Content

Let $X$ and $X'$ be standardized abelian Cayley graphs with Heuberger matrices $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.
If $M_{X'}$ is obtained by permuting the columns of $M_X$, then $X=X'$.

## Proof

Let $H_X$ and $H_{X'}$ be the subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}^m$ generated by the columns of $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.

Permuting the columns does not change their $\mathbb{Z}$-span.
Therefore, $H_X=H_{X'}$.

By [[sacg.matrix-realization]], the graph realized by a Heuberger matrix depends only on the subgroup generated by its columns and the standardized generating set
\[\{H_X\pm e_1,\ldots,H_X\pm e_m\}.\]

Thus, $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$ realize the same standardized abelian Cayley graph, so $X=X'$.
