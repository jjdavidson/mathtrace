# Main Content

Let $X$ and $X'$ be standardized abelian Cayley graphs with Heuberger matrices $M_X=(y_1\ \cdots\ y_r)$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.
Let $i,j\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$ with $i\neq j$, and let $a\in\mathbb{Z}$.
If $M_{X'}$ is obtained by replacing $y_j$ with $y_j+ay_i$, then $X=X'$.

## Proof

Let $H_X$ and $H_{X'}$ be the subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}^m$ generated by the columns of $M_X$ and $M_{X'}$, respectively.

Every column of $M_{X'}$ belongs to $H_X$. In particular, $y_j+ay_i\in H_X$. Therefore, $H_{X'}\subseteq H_X$.

Conversely, $y_i$ belongs to $H_{X'}$, and $y_j=(y_j+ay_i)-ay_i$. Thus, $y_j\in H_{X'}$.
Every other column of $M_X$ is also a column of $M_{X'}$, so $H_X\subseteq H_{X'}$. Hence, $H_X=H_{X'}$.

By [[sacg.matrix-realization]], the matrices consequently realize the same standardized abelian Cayley graph. Therefore, $X=X'$.
