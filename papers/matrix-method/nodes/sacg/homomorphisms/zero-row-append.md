# Main Content

Let
\[
M_X =
\begin{pmatrix}
    y_{11} & \cdots & y_{1r}\\
    \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
    y_{m1} & \cdots & y_{mr}
\end{pmatrix}
\]
be a Heuberger matrix for a standardized abelian Cayley graph $X$.
Let $Y$ be the standardized abelian Cayley graph with Heuberger matrix

\[
M_Y =
\begin{pmatrix}
    y_{11} & \cdots & y_{1r}\\
    \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
    y_{m1} & \cdots & y_{mr}\\
    0 & \cdots & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then there is a graph homomorphism
\[
(M_X)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_X \xrightarrow{\circledcirc}(M_Y)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_Y.
\]

Thus, appending a zero row to a Heuberger matrix produces a graph homomorphism from the original graph to the graph associated with the enlarged matrix.

## Proof

Let $e_1,\dots,e_m$ be the standard basis of $\mathbb{Z}^m$, and let $e'_1,\dots,e'_{m+1}$ be the standard basis of $\mathbb{Z}^{m+1}$.

Define $\tau\colon\mathbb{Z}^m\to\mathbb{Z}^{m+1}$ by $\tau(e_i)=e'_i$ for every $i\in\{1,\dots,m\}$.
If $y_j$ is the $j$th column of $M_X$, then
\[
\tau(y_j)=
\begin{pmatrix}
    y_j\\
    0
\end{pmatrix},
\]
which is the $j$th column of $M_Y$.
Thus, $\tau$ maps every column of $M_X$ into the subgroup generated by the columns of $M_Y$.

Moreover, every standard basis vector $e_i$ maps to the standard basis vector $e'_i$.
The result follows from [[sacg.homomorphisms.criterion]].
