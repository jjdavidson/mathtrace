# Main Content

Let $y_1,\dots,y_r\in\mathbb{Z}^m$, let $j\in\{1,\dots,r\}$, and let $a\in\mathbb{Z}$.
Define standardized abelian Cayley graphs $X$ and $Y$ by $M_X=(y_1\ \cdots\ ay_j\ \cdots\ y_r)$ and $M_Y=(y_1\ \cdots\ y_j\ \cdots\ y_r)$,  respectively.
Then the identity map on the standard basis induces a graph homomorphism
\[(M_X)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_X\xrightarrow{\circledcirc}(M_Y)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_Y.\]
Thus, replacing a column $ay_j$ by $y_j$ produces a graph homomorphism from the original standardized abelian Cayley graph to the reduced one.

## Proof

Let $H_X$ and $H_Y$ be the subgroups generated by the columns of $M_X$ and $M_Y$, respectively.

Since $ay_j\in H_Y$ and every other column of $M_X$ is also a column of $M_Y$, we have $H_X\subseteq H_Y$.
Let $\tau\colon\mathbb{Z}^m\to\mathbb{Z}^m$ be the identity map.
Then $\tau(H_X)\subseteq H_Y$, and $\tau(e_i)=e_i$ for every $i\in\{1,\dots,m\}$.
Therefore, [[sacg.homomorphisms.criterion]] gives the required graph homomorphism.
