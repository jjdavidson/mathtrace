# Main Content

Let
\[
M_X =
\begin{pmatrix}
    y_{11} & \cdots & y_{1r}\\
    y_{21} & \cdots & y_{2r}\\
    y_{31} & \cdots & y_{3r}\\
    \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
    y_{m1} & \cdots & y_{mr}
\end{pmatrix}
\]
be a Heuberger matrix for a standardized abelian Cayley graph $X$, where $m\geq 2$.
Let $Y$ be the standardized abelian Cayley graph with Heuberger matrix
\[
M_Y =
\begin{pmatrix}
    y_{11}+y_{21} & \cdots & y_{1r}+y_{2r}\\
    y_{31} & \cdots & y_{3r}\\
    \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
    y_{m1} & \cdots & y_{mr}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Then there is a graph homomorphism
\[(M_X)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_X\xrightarrow{\circledcirc}(M_Y)^{\mathrm{SACG}}_Y.\]
In other words, collapsing two rows by adding them produces a graph homomorphism.

## Proof

Let $e_1,\dots,e_m$ be the standard basis of $\mathbb{Z}^m$, and let $e'_1,\dots,e'_{m-1}$ be the standard basis of $\mathbb{Z}^{m-1}$.
Define $\tau\colon\mathbb{Z}^m\to\mathbb{Z}^{m-1}$ by $\tau(e_1)=\tau(e_2)=e'_1$ and $\tau(e_i)=e'_{i-1}$ for every $i\in\{3,\dots,m\}$.

If $y_j$ is the $j$th column of $M_X$, then
\[\tau(y_j) = (y_{1j}+y_{2j})e'_1+\sum_{i=3}^m y_{ij}e'_{i-1},\]
which is the $j$th column of $M_Y$.
Thus, $\tau$ maps every column of $M_X$ into the subgroup generated by the columns of $M_Y$.

Moreover, every standard basis vector of $\mathbb{Z}^m$ maps to a standard basis vector of $\mathbb{Z}^{m-1}$.
The result follows from [[sacg.homomorphisms.criterion]].
